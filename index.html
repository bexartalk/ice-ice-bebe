Ice Ice Bebé

Help Jose run home to Mom's!

    How to play

    Phone: tap the game or ⤒ Jump to jump, tap again in the air to double-jump. Tap Throw to toss a burrito.

    Keyboard: Space / ↑ / W jump (twice for a double jump) · X / F throw · P or Esc pause · R restart the level · M sound · Enter next level.

    🌯 A burrito bonks an agent (he sits down dizzy). Land on an agent's cap to stomp him flat and bounce. Hop the cones, dodge the SUVs, grab ☕ coffee for speed and a 🩴 flip-flop for a shield. Caught? Back to the last 🚩 checkpoint. Nobody gets hurt.

    📲 To install on iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
